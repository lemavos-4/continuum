## Frequently asked questions

### How does Continuum sync across my devices?

Your knowledge graph syncs natively across every device where you're signed in. No plugins or manual setup — just open the app and everything is there. When offline, changes are saved locally and synced automatically once you're back online.

### Can I use Continuum offline?

Yes. Continuum is a PWA with full offline support. You can read and edit your notes without a connection, and your changes sync the next time you're online.

### How do mentions and entities work?

Type `@` to mention people or projects and `#` to reference topics. Every mention becomes a living link that strengthens your knowledge graph, so related notes resurface when you need them.

### What is the Score System?

Notes and entities earn relevance based on how you interact with them. The most important items float to the top automatically — no manual tagging required.

### How do I manage my subscription?

Go to Subscription from the menu to view your plan, upgrade, or manage billing at any time.

### How can I delete my account or data?

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at [contact@continuum.onl](mailto:contact@continuum.onl). We'll respond promptly.
